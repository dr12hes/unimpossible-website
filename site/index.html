What AI is already doing for businesses like yours

None of this needs new software your team has to learn. It runs inside the email, accounts and website you already have, and a person approves anything that matters.

Quotes out the same morning

An enquiry arrives by email. The assistant reads it, pulls the right items from the price list, drafts the quote in the firm's usual wording and leaves it for the owner to check and send. What used to wait for Friday afternoon goes out before coffee.

Invoices that file themselves

Supplier invoices are read, matched to the order and posted to the accounts package. Anything that doesn't match is set aside with a note saying why. The bookkeeper looks at the exceptions, not the pile.

Questions answered at eleven at night

An assistant on the website answers from the business's own information, not the internet: opening hours, what's included, how long it takes. When someone is ready, it books the call. Enquiries that would have gone to a competitor stay put.

The weekly report nobody had time to write

Jobs, cash and overdue items pulled together every Monday in plain English, with the three things that need a decision at the top. The team starts the week knowing where it stands.

How we work

  1. 1

    Understand

    A free 30-minute conversation about what's slowing the business down. If it's worth going further, a two-week review of how work actually moves through your inboxes, systems and people, ending in a ranked list of where AI would pay, and where it wouldn't.

  2. 2

    Build

    The first win built in weeks, not months, inside the tools you already use, at a price agreed before we start. You see it working as it takes shape, not in a slide deck at the end.

  3. 3

    Run

    We can look after it, improve it and send you a plain-English monthly note, or hand it over completely. Either way the code, the accounts and the documentation are yours.
