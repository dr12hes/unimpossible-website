Privacy

UNimpossible Consultants Limited, Watton, Norfolk, is the data controller for this website. We do not use advertising or third-party analytics. Our optional AI chat uses a session identifier to keep a conversation together.

When you contact us

If you use the form or email hello@unimpossible.co.uk, we receive what you send: your name, company, email address and message. We use it to reply to you and, if we go on to work together, to do the work. We keep enquiries for as long as the conversation is live and for up to two years afterwards, then delete them. Website enquiries are stored in our Optaro Core system and the team receives an email notification through Postmark. We don't add you to a mailing list or sell your details. Service providers process only the information needed to deliver the service.

The website read and the audit

When you enter a website address in the read on our home page, our server fetches a few public pages from that site and sends their text to Anthropic's Claude API, which produces the read. We keep the address, the fetched text and the result to complete the audit and reuse recent results. If you ask for the full audit we also keep your email address and name, send you one confirmation email, fetch public pages from the competitor sites we find, and send you the report as a link. A copy of the report goes to hello@unimpossible.co.uk so we can follow up if you reply. None of it is sold or shared, and we'll delete it if you ask.

Audit confirmation and delivery emails are sent through Postmark. Competitor discovery uses Tavily search. We send these providers only the information needed to perform those steps.

The AI assistant

The chat is an AI assistant, not a live member of staff. Optaro Core stores conversation messages to provide answers and lets our team review enquiries. AI providers process messages to generate responses. If you provide contact details, Core may save them with your enquiry so the team can follow up. Please use the enquiry form when you need a reply, and do not share passwords, payment details or confidential customer information in chat. We apply the enquiry retention period above to conversations and contact details, and you can request deletion by email.

Hosting

The site is served from servers we run in the EU and UK. Like any web server they keep short-lived access logs (IP address, pages requested, browser type) for security and fault-finding, which rotate automatically.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it or delete it, at any time: hello@unimpossible.co.uk. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
