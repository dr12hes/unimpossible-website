Search and referral measurement

We use Google Search Console to view Google's reports about our website's search visibility and traffic. We also keep daily counts of requests to our public pages that arrive with a recognised search-engine or AI-assistant referral. These counters use no analytics cookies or visitor identifiers. They record the time, page and a source category, not your IP address or the referring page's full address. Known automated clients and our test requests are excluded, but these counts are not a measure of unique people. We keep the separate referral records for up to 36 days and daily totals for up to 400 days.
