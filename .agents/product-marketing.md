# Unimpossible site brief

Source: https://unimpossible.co.uk/ public HTML and /site.js?v=20261021-leads fetched 24 October 2026. These are published business claims, not independent validation. This brief is a sourced working draft; it does not invent customer research or claim fresh owner approval.

Audience: businesses exploring practical AI/software help; Norfolk positioning is explicit. More specific segments and buyer research are unconfirmed.
Offer: free 30-minute conversation with an engineer; separate free one-hour AI session for Norfolk Chambers members. Preserve eligibility and duration. Delivery: understand, build, run; examples concern operational business work. Do not turn examples or global platform experience into quantified customer results.
Voice: direct, practical, plain English. Main journey: homepage CTA scrolls to enquiry form. Public script sends an enquiry and promises a reply within one working day; it does not show a booking calendar. Actual delivery and reply performance were not tested.
Contact: name/email required; company/message optional in HTML and client validation. Primary desired outcome: relevant enquiries leading to useful conversations. Qualification rules, current conversion rates and usable page/query baseline are not established in this review.
Learning: current homepage description was retained after a publish/undo proof. Operational success is not evidence of search uplift. Do not remove the Chambers offer from its section or imply it applies to every visitor.
