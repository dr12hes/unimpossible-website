# Unimpossible cro

Read [the shared evidence and approval contract](../../skill-contract.md) before applying this workflow. Read the relevant site's approved brief; for our own site use [the Unimpossible brief](../../product-marketing.md).

Identify page purpose, audience, primary action and actual destination behaviour. Examine promise, proof, hierarchy, next-step clarity and avoidable friction. Separate observed usability issues from conversion hypotheses; never label a change as an immediate guaranteed win.
For forms inspect labels, required flags, validation and success/error behaviour without submitting real enquiries unless authorised. Explain requested information, visibly distinguish optional fields, and preserve accessible labels, privacy information and spam protections. Do not impose universal field-count penalties or remove qualification fields merely to increase submissions. Use established form guidance: https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/forms/instructions/ .
For chat or multiple contact channels, assess total qualified enquiries with duplicate handling; channel switching is not growth. Recommend the smallest justified change. Report field purpose, exact proposal, possible loss of useful information and downstream measurement. Do not add new lead enrichment, tracking or external services merely to complete a review.
