# Unimpossible product-marketing

Read [the shared evidence and approval contract](../../skill-contract.md) before applying this workflow. Read the relevant site's approved brief; for our own site use [the Unimpossible brief](../../product-marketing.md).

Read the site's approved brief and current pages before drafting a new brief. Separate owner-approved facts, public claims and inferred audience needs; a published claim is not independent proof. Record source/date and unresolved questions.
Keep audience, problem, offer, differentiators, customer language, primary action and material eligibility conditions. Preserve special offers (including Chambers eligibility) without generalising them to all visitors. Never invent testimonials, results or guarantees. Keep each customer's facts separate. Update only facts relevant to this task; do not require a full positioning exercise for a point edit.
